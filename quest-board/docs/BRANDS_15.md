# Fifteen new brands (LEAD's draft for Cal, 2026-11-06)

Cal: "Let's build a Naruto inspired clothing brand, and a maplestory inspired clothing brand from their most popular cash shop
items, then let's do a video game inspired brand, then a fantasy one, then a cyberpunk one. And also make 10 more brands that
come after these. Draft up a list for my approval and then once approved, send to ROOMs to begin generation".

Same formula as the five in the Shop now (HANGANG, DIAL-UP, OVERPASS, VENOM ROSE, ALTITUDE): a name, a playful tagline, a
sub-line, a palette, a painted banner with a stylized logo, and 15 pieces (3 hats, 2 accessories, 4 tops, 3 bottoms, 3 shoes),
one weekly drop each. One Higgsfield sheet per piece, every angle, both bodies (ROOMS, from Cal's Chrome, Unlimited on).

**All original.** "Inspired by" means the genre and the feel. No real logos, no characters or their exact outfits, no village
or clan symbols, no copied items. A fan should read the vibe at a glance; nothing should be a copy.

Brands 6 to 10 are Cal's five themes. Brands 11 to 20 are ROOMS's ten from `BRANDS_PROPOSAL.md` (still waiting for Cal), with
GRIDLOCK (techwear) swapped for WHITEOUT, because CHROME RAIN now covers that look.

## Cal's five: pieces

**SMOKE BOMB (ninja anime).** Logo: a stylized smoke puff with a thrown star cut through it (its own crest, no village mark).
Hats: cloth forehead band with a plain engraved metal plate (SMOKE BOMB's own crest); half-face cowl mask (mask slot); spiky
hair-tie headwrap. Accessories: arm and leg wrap tape set; scroll pouch on a hip strap. Tops: high-collar zip jacket, ink black
with ember-orange panels; mesh undershirt under a sleeveless wrap top; charcoal utility vest with scroll pockets; hooded short
cloak with torn hem. Bottoms: wrapped-shin cargo pants; hakama-style wide trousers; shorts over mesh leggings. Shoes:
open-toe shinobi sandals; split-toe tabi boots; wrapped running shoes.

**GACHA POP (MMO cash shop).** Logo: a capsule toy opening in a burst of stars. Hats: cat-ear hoodie hood; oversized bunny-ear
beanie; giant satin ribbon bow. Accessories: small feathered angel wings (back, as a neck piece); heart-shaped sunglasses.
Tops: pastel school-uniform blazer with ribbon; fluffy animal-onesie top (bear); star-print oversized hoodie; frilled sailor
blouse. Bottoms: pleated skirt with star trim (women's); puffy shorts with suspenders; pastel overalls. Shoes: chunky
platform Mary Janes; fluffy slipper boots; light-up glitter sneakers.

**SAVE POINT (retro video games).** Logo: a pixel floppy disk with a little flag on it. Hats: pixel-heart beanie; gaming
headset (hat slot); backwards cap with a pixel coin badge. Accessories: cartridge pendant necklace; pixel shades. Tops: arcade
varsity jacket with a joystick patch; controller-button graphic tee; "player 2" ringer tee; health-bar knit sweater. Bottoms:
pixel-camo cargo pants; track pants with a d-pad stripe; denim shorts with patch stickers. Shoes: high-top sneakers with
pixel flames; chunky retro runners; slip-ons with a checkpoint flag print.

**BARD & BLADE (high fantasy).** Logo: a lute crossed with a short sword inside a laurel. Hats: pointed wizard hat with a
brass buckle; hooded ranger mantle; silver leaf circlet (its own design). Accessories: leather bracers; a crystal on a cord.
Tops: green adventurer's tunic with a belt; quilted gambeson jacket; velvet bard doublet with puffed sleeves; long travel cloak
with a clasp. Bottoms: laced leather trousers; layered skirt with a tool belt (women's); tucked breeches. Shoes: tall
cuffed boots; soft elven slippers; hobnailed walking boots.

**CHROME RAIN (cyberpunk).** Logo: a chrome raindrop with a glowing circuit line through it. Hats: LED visor; neon mohawk
helmet; hood with a glowing rim. Accessories: chrome cyber-arm sleeve (one arm); mirrored shades. Tops: holographic
high-collar trench; glowing-seam bomber jacket; cropped mesh top with a harness; armoured street vest. Bottoms: glossy black
cargo pants with neon piping; split-panel techno skirt (women's); armoured knee joggers. Shoes: light-up sole sneakers;
platform buckle boots; chrome-toe combat boots.

puffy snow pants; stretch ski leggings (women's); corduroy lodge trousers. Shoes: moon boots; lace-up après boots; shearling
slippers.

## Size of the job

15 brands x 15 pieces = 225 cosmetics, plus 15 banners and 15 logos. At ROOMS's pace, one weekly drop each gives months of
runway; generation can go brand by brand in drop order, starting with SMOKE BOMB.
