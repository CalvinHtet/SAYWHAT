# Exercise demo checklist

Each demo shows the start and end of one rep. This is what each pair has to show, taken
from coaching references. When a demo is regenerated, check it against this list and look
at the two frames blended together: the equipment must not move, only the person.

| Exercise | Start | End | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barbell bench press | Bar on mid or lower chest, forearms vertical | Arms vertical, bar over the shoulders | [Barbell Logic](https://barbell-logic.com/how-to-bench-press-setup-safety-bar-path/) |
| Close-grip bench | Same, elbows tucked | Same | [Barbell Logic](https://barbell-logic.com/how-to-bench-press-setup-safety-bar-path/) |
| Incline dumbbell press | Dumbbells beside upper chest | Arms straight over shoulders | Same pressing path as the bench |
| Dips | Elbows locked at the top | Upper arms about parallel to the floor, slight forward lean | [StrongLifts](https://stronglifts.com/dips/), [Gravitus](https://gravitus.com/guides/exercises/dip) |
| Overhead press | Bar at collarbone | Arms locked, bar over the head | [Muscle & Strength](https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/shoulders) |
| Lateral raise | Arms at the sides | Arms out to shoulder height, elbows slightly bent | General coaching cue; no single reference found |
| Rear delt fly | Hinged forward, arms hanging | Arms out to the sides, neck relaxed | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rear_delt_raise) |
| Shrug | Shoulders relaxed | Shoulders straight up toward the ears | [Liftosaur](https://www.liftosaur.com/exercises/dumbbell-shrug) |
| Barbell curl | Bar at the thighs | Bar curled to the chest, elbows still | General coaching cue |
| Incline curl | Arms hanging, full stretch | Full squeeze, bench 45 to 60 degrees | [Gravitus](https://gravitus.com/guides/exercises/incline-dumbbell-curl) |
| Hammer curl | Palms facing each other at the sides | Same grip at the shoulders | General coaching cue |
| Triceps pushdown | High pulley, elbows pinned | Arms straight down | [Liftosaur](https://www.liftosaur.com/exercises/cable-triceps-pushdown) |
| Overhead triceps extension | Weight behind the head, elbows up | Arms straight overhead, elbows not flared | [Liftosaur](https://www.liftosaur.com/exercises/dumbbell-triceps-extension) |
| Lat pulldown | Thighs under the pad, arms up | Bar to the chest, elbows down | [Liftosaur](https://liftosaur.com/exercises/cable-lat-pulldown) |
| Seated cable row | Arms forward, torso tall | Handle to the stomach | [Liftosaur](https://liftosaur.com/exercises/cable-seated-row) |
| Face pull | Rope at face height, arms forward | Rope to the forehead, elbows high | [StrengthLog](https://www.strengthlog.com/face-pull/) |
| Deadlift | Bar over mid foot, shins near the bar, hips above the knees | Standing tall | [Starting Strength](https://startingstrength.com/training/common-deadlift-error-hips-too-low-and-the-bar-forward) |
| Back squat | Standing tall | Thighs at least parallel, chest up | [PMC review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4725067/) |
| Romanian deadlift | Standing, bar at the thighs | Hips back, flat back, soft knees | General coaching cue |
| Leg press | Legs nearly straight | Knees bent toward the chest | [Beachbody](https://www.beachbodyondemand.com/blog/?p=90897) |
| Lying leg curl | Chest and thighs on the pad, pad on the lower calf | Heels curled toward the hips | [Brainscape](https://brainscape.com/flashcards/practicum-6-lower-body-14468811/packs/21687358) |
| Standing calf raise | Heels down | Up on the toes, full contraction | [Liftosaur](https://liftosaur.com/exercises/dumbbell-standing-calf-raise) |
| Walking lunge | Standing | Back knee just above the floor | [Brainscape](https://brainscape.com/flashcards/practicum-6-lower-body-14468811/packs/21687358) |
| Hanging leg raise | Hanging, legs down | Straight legs to hip height | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leg_raise) |
| Plank | Elbows under shoulders, straight line head to heels | Held | [NASM](https://blog.nasm.org/standard-plank-with-variations) |
| Cable crunch | Kneeling, high pulley, rope by the ears | Spine curled, elbows toward the knees, hips still | [Liftosaur](https://liftosaur.com/exercises/cable-crunch), [Gravitus](https://gravitus.com/guides/exercises/kneeling-cable-crunch) |
| Russian twist | Torso about 45 degrees, feet just off the floor, turned one way | Same seat, turned the other way | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_twist), [Healthline](https://www.healthline.com/health/russian-twist) |
| Back extension | Bent down at the hips on a 45 degree bench | Body in one straight line | General coaching cue |
| Farmer carry | Tall posture, weights at the sides, one leg forward | Other leg forward | General coaching cue; no single reference found |
