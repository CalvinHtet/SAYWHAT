# Relic pictures, for Cal's Mac Claude

Cal, 2026-11-10: "create multiple tiers of relics or low, mid, high, and legendary relics. Just put a placeholder picture there for
now but leave a note for my claude on mac to take over and generate images later on." The list below is Cal's approved list (SPEC6).

The relic tiers are built (`app/src/29y_free.js`, `RELICS2`; buffs in `ECON.relicBuff`). Each relic shows a gem drawn in code until
its picture exists. Same rules as every other piece of art: Cal's Chrome with the Unlimited toggle on, no credits, original designs
only, Cal approves first.

- One icon per relic: `art/app/ui_relic2_<id>.webp`, 256x256, transparent, the game's painted style, the object alone, no text, no
  border (the game draws the tier border: bronze, silver, gold, or white and purple with wings for legendary).
- Low relics are household heirlooms. Mid, high and legendary are fantasy, dungeon, anime and otherworld artifacts.
