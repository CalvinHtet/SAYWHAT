in the spirit of the cosmetic shops of 2D side-scrolling MMOs. Original: no game's name, items, mascots or UI; nothing repaints a
specific cash-shop item. REFERENCES.md row: style.

| Brand | Sub | Tagline | Palette | Accent |

sides; WISHBOX in soft bubble letters with a star for the dot-free O. **Banner:** a pastel cloud shop counter stacked with glowing
