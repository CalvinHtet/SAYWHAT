# GILDED (Boutique, takes CHROME RAIN's held slot): draft pieces (ROOMS, 2026-11-18)

Concept approved by Cal 2026-11-18 ("approve all", via LEAD): baroque streetwear, "Too much is the right amount".
Palette change, technical: the concept said black, antique gold, emerald. Every piece is painted on a pure-green screen
and keyed, so emerald fabric would be cut out with the background. GILDED is black, antique gold and ivory, with small
deep-sapphire gems as the accent.

Cal's standing rules: nothing plain (every line names two or more details), original designs only (invented crests and
scrollwork, no real fashion-house monograms or baroque logos), no cultural dress (European-baroque ornament only, no
dragon, phoenix or cloud brocades, no national garments), adults only. The bustier is a `bare` top and goes through
QA's coverage gate.

Boutique tier (LEAD prices it with ECONOMY.md); the floor's "lots of detail" bar applies.
