# GILDED (Boutique, takes CHROME RAIN's held slot): draft pieces (ROOMS, 2026-11-18; revised after QA a6cf9f6)

Concept approved by Cal 2026-11-18 ("approve all", via LEAD): baroque streetwear, "Too much is the right amount".
Palette change, technical: the concept said black, antique gold, emerald. Every piece is painted on a pure-green screen
and keyed, so emerald fabric would be cut out with the background. GILDED is black, antique gold and ivory, with small
deep-sapphire gems as the accent.

Cal's standing rules: nothing plain (every line names two or more details), original designs only, no cultural dress,
adults only. The bustier is a `bare` top (QA's coverage gate); the mules use the tiptoe `heel` base.

Trade-dress guard (QA a6cf9f6): GILDED must not read as any fashion house's black-and-gold baroque signature. So:
invented leaf-and-filigree ornament only; no faces or heads in any crest, medallion or button; no Greek-key borders; no
repeating baroque print tile (gold reads as brocade texture woven into the cloth); no chain collar with a medallion; the
logo is never a standalone laurel roundel. Velvet appears once (the beret).
