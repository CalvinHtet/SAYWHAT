# Pet clothes: 48 concepts (ROOMS, 2026-11-18), for QA's design review before any paint

Cal 2026-11-18 (via LEAD): "12 per pet", then "Dogs too": Chopstick and Pluto share one line of 12 (one piece fits both dogs,
placed on each dog's own anchors and scaled to that dog; anchors in data/pet_art.json). Three per slot per pet: head, neck or base, body, aura. Each piece is one Higgsfield
image made for its own pet's shape, placed on that pet's per-frame anchors in all 11 frames (crying included), no back art.
Each follows its pet's colours where it makes sense (Cal's colour change, same day): wolf pup charcoal with icy cyan, wyvern
navy with amber-gold, fox-slime plum with hot pink; the dogs' line is warm and homey (caramel, cream, cherry red, navy), so it
reads apart from the three creatures. Pluto's painted grey vest stays under body pieces (they cover it).

Rules: never plain (every line names two or more details), original designs only (no named character's gear, no brand
logos, no letters on anything), no cultural dress, nothing green (keyed). Pieces sit over the pet, so pink and magenta are
fine here (the magenta-body rule is only for people's clothes).
