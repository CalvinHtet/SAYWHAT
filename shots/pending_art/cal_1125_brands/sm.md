# sm SAYMØRE: to 8 per category (LEAD 11-28: brands in drop order; SAYMØRE drops 12-21)

Brand look (BRANDS_15, from Cal's covers): bass and night culture, "Trust the motion". Night black, flare orange (the halo and
outline type), pulse cyan (neon piping that glows), bone, ash grey. The Ø mark (circle cut by a slash) is Cal's own mark and may
appear as a small badge; no other letters, words, kanji or glyphs anywhere. Glow, neon piping, mesh, gloss, rave hardware. Never
plain, never cute; sexy pieces welcome (skin fine, never nude).

track, vest, grille), bottom 3 (cargo, nightcargo, shorts), shoes 3 (hightop, runner, boots), gloves 0.

  repaint (clean the sleeve) or a retire-and-replace.

  cyan stripe each); sm_halo Neon Halo is a bare ring. Suggest retiring sm_shorts and sm_nightcargo with their replacements below
