# oc OIL & CANVAS: to 8 per category (LEAD 11-27: brands in drop order; OIL & CANVAS drops Wed 12-02)

Brand look: art-school streetwear. Cream canvas, raw indigo, cadmium orange-red, gesso white, charcoal black. Paint splatter, brush
sweeps, gesso drips and palette-knife scrapes on real street shapes (hoodies, cargos, workwear, sneakers, chains). Streetwear first:
no painter props, no berets, no smocks, no brushes or palettes carried or worn as charms (streetwear audit 11-27). Never plain, never
cute: every piece gets real detail. No text, no logos, no real brand marks.
